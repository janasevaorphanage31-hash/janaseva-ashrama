# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

1. **Individual Donors & Meal Sponsors (Primary):** Working professionals, families, and philanthropists in Bengaluru and across India looking to sponsor wholesome meals (Annadana), celebrate birthdays/anniversaries, or provide monthly grocery and education support to destitute children.
2. **Birthday & Memorial Celebrants:** Families who want to celebrate their special days with meaningful impact by visiting the Turahalli ashrama in person, cutting cake with the children, and serving festive banana-leaf lunches.
3. **Corporate CSR Heads & HR Teams:** Companies looking for verified Section 135 CSR partnerships, employee engagement volunteering, or infrastructure sponsorships with full 80G/12AA compliance.
4. **Global Indian Diaspora (NRIs):** Donors seeking verified, transparent grassroots child welfare institutions in India with seamless digital giving and WhatsApp accountability.

## Product Purpose

Janaseva Ashrama is the official digital portal for a licensed children's home in Bangalore supporting 25 orphaned, semi-orphaned, and destitute boys (ages 07–18). The platform enables donors to sponsor daily nutrition, education kits, medical care, and celebrate milestones with complete transparency, instant 80G tax-exemption receipts, and direct WhatsApp communication with ashrama caregivers.

## Positioning

Direct, unmediated, 100% transparent grassroots ashrama care in Turahalli, Bengaluru South. Unlike large aggregator NGOs where funds disappear into overhead, 100% of public sponsorships directly feed and educate 25 specific resident boys. Every celebration is backed by verified government licenses (Juvenile Justice Act registration `KA18CH0242`, Form 10AC 80G tax exemption, and MCA CSR registration `CSR00078800`).

## Operating Context

- **Traffic Sources:** Heavy mobile traffic driven by Meta Ads (Instagram/Facebook), Google Ads, WhatsApp referrals, and local Bangalore search queries ("orphanage near me", "annadana bangalore").
- **Payment Rituals:** Fast UPI payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Net Banking, and Cards via Reserve Bank of India compliant Razorpay gateway.
- **Immediate Post-Payment Action:** Instant digital 80G receipt generation and direct WhatsApp notification to the donor with photos and prayer updates.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Capabilities:**
  - Zero-friction one-click donation checkout supporting pre-set and custom donation amounts starting from ₹11.
  - Multi-cause sponsorship (Daily Annadana, Birthday Feasts, Monthly Groceries, Education & School Kits, Health & Medical Care).
  - Birthday & special day campus visit scheduling with meal selection.
  - Telecaller and caregiver WhatsApp CRM integration.
  - Automated 80G tax receipt PDF generation and download.
- **Constraints:**
  - Child safeguarding: Strict adherence to JJ Act guidelines; no invasive photography or video exploitation of children.
  - Performance: Mobile pages must render under 1.5 seconds on 4G networks.
  - Zero payment drop-off: Checkout amount must stay strictly synchronized between homepage selection and payment gateway.

## Product Principles

1. **Radical Transparency Over Aggressive Marketing:** Real receipts, verified license numbers, clear meal breakdowns, and direct caregiver access beat glossy claims.
2. **Dignity of the Child is Paramount:** Every child is presented as a capable student and future leader, not a victim for pity donations.
3. **Frictionless Donor Journey:** Donors must never be trapped in confusing forms; select amount ➔ tap Pay ➔ instant WhatsApp receipt.
4. **Mobile & Ads Readiness:** Every pixel must perform flawlessly on mobile screens, specifically optimized for Meta & Google Ads traffic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
