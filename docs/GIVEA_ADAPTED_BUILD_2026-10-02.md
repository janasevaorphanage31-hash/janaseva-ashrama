# GiveA-inspired Janaseva build

Implemented without copying GiveA branding/content: impact catalogue search/filter, Today priority labels, stronger Your Impact summary, donation FAQ, pre-checkout Gift an Impact intent, verified gift readiness, post-payment Impact Journey, and homepage flow alignment.

## Deliberately not copied
- GiveA names, visual identity or copy
- Unsupported impact claims
- Large marketplace-style catalogue
- Any promise of media updates before Janaseva has approved media

## Operational note
Impact item prices and accounting meanings remain admin/finance controlled. Demo data must not be published as real impact.
